All 30 MP3 files are copied byte for byte. No audio files were trimmed, normalized or re-encoded. Runtime playback can repitch samples, change gain and apply release envelopes. No endorsement by the author or Tone.js is implied.

This compact single-sample-per-root bank covers MIDI 21 (A0) through 108 (C8), at three-semitone intervals. A nearest-root player needs at most one semitone of repitching for the intervening piano keys. It is not the original full 16-velocity-layer SFZ instrument.
